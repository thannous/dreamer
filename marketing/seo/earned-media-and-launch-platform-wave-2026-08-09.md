and voice-journaling comparisons proved heavily duplicated or product-owned.

## HARO execution pack

Use the same truthful founder bio and exclusions in
`marketing/seo/media-source-platform-pack-2026-08-09.md`.

For a future query, the response must contain:

1. a direct 60–140 word answer based on Thanh Chau's first-hand experience;
2. one concrete product-development example or trade-off;
3. disclosure that Thanh founded Noctalia when the app is relevant;
4. one public Noctalia source URL only when it supports the requested fact;
5. no attachment, keyword anchor, backlink request or unrelated product pitch.

Reject medical, diagnostic, therapeutic, sleep-disorder, trauma, predictive
dream or scientific-expert queries. Also reject anonymous requests that ask for
private user data, unpublished product information, payment or guaranteed
promotion.
