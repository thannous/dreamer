# Editorial discovery — wave 48 — 2026-08-13

This pass tests seven previously untracked journaling or dream-app pages for a
credible direct-domain backlink route. Search discovery and rendered-page
inspection used the user-designated real Chrome session. The stop order was:
page value, citation pattern, operator accountability, then contact enrichment.
No email, form, account, attachment, payment, publication or product data was
used.

## Qualification rule

A high-authority host or a visible submission form is not sufficient. A route
must expose an indexable editorial page, a direct external product-domain
citation precedent, an accountable non-Russian operator and an official public
contact. A store-only, internal-profile or plain-text mention cannot improve
`noctalia.app` domain authority.

## Result

All seven routes are recorded as P2 rejections. AppGuias and Jenova passed the
accountability and non-Russian checks, but failed the backlink-value or brand
fit gates. The other five failed before contact enrichment; they are not being
treated as trusted outreach targets. No message or submission was prepared or
sent, and no backlink or DR gain is claimed.

The next discovery pass should prioritize independent journalists, universities,
sleep-research organizations and established editorial publishers that already
link comparable product domains directly. Product-owned comparison pages,
automated app catalogs and store-only citation patterns should remain excluded.
