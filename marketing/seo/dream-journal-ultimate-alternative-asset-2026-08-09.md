## Evidence and comparison boundaries

The page uses only current public official product pages, store listings,
privacy disclosures and existing verified Noctalia product facts. It gives
Dream Journal Ultimate credit for iOS, Dream Wall, similar-dream discovery,
its broader symbol surface and structured lucid-dreaming tools. It gives
Noctalia credit only for its Android-first voice-to-reflection workflow,
visual cards, recurring symbols, guided private reflection and published
privacy/provider disclosures.

It does not claim that Noctalia has equivalent scale, supports iOS, imports a
full portable journal, is universally cheaper, is medically safer, or provides
better privacy in every respect. Store ratings, review counts and update dates
are labeled as an August 9, 2026 snapshot.
