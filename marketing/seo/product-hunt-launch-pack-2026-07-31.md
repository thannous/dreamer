# Noctalia Product Hunt launch pack

Evidence date: 2026-07-31. This is a launch-ready local dossier, not a published Product Hunt draft. Creating the external draft, uploading the video to YouTube, scheduling the launch and sending launch messages each remain separate external actions requiring explicit approval.

## Current official constraints

- A personal Product Hunt account is required; a company account cannot post, vote or comment.
- A new personal account normally needs to be at least one week old before it can post.
- Product Hunt recommends that makers post their own product; a third-party hunter is not required.
- The primary URL must point directly to the product, not to a press or blog page.
- Gallery images should be `1270 × 760`; at least two are needed for a visible gallery.
- Product Hunt currently accepts YouTube URLs for video, not a direct MP4 URL. The video must not be private.
- A draft can be created without choosing a launch date. A launch can be scheduled up to 30 days ahead and goes live at `12:01 AM` Pacific Time.
- Ask people for feedback and conversation, never for upvotes.

### Description

The copy below stays below 260 characters so it fits both the older Help Center limit and the newer launch-guide field.

> Noctalia is an Android-first dream journal for fast voice or text capture, structured AI-assisted reflection, dream imagery, guided questions and pattern review. It supports five languages and is built for reflection—not diagnosis or prediction.

### First maker comment

> Hi Product Hunt 👋 I’m Thanh, the maker of Noctalia.
>
> I built Noctalia because dream details disappear quickly after waking, while most journaling workflows ask for too much effort at exactly that moment. The app starts with fast voice or text capture, then turns the entry into a structured record that can be revisited through possible symbols, emotions, themes, generated imagery and contextual follow-up questions.
>
> One boundary matters to me: Noctalia is a reflection tool, not an oracle or a medical product. It does not diagnose, predict events or claim that every symbol has one universal meaning. I have tried to make that distinction visible in both the product and its methodology.
>
> Noctalia is available on Android in English, French, Spanish, German and Italian. There is a free Dreamer plan, with an optional Plus subscription for higher limits.
>
> I would especially value feedback on two things: does the voice-first workflow feel clear from the launch page, and are the limits of the AI reflection explained plainly enough?

## Relative launch timeline

### D-21 to D-8

1. Verify the personal Product Hunt account, exact username, onboarding completion and posting eligibility.
2. Participate only where there is a genuine product question to answer. Do not manufacture activity or mass-follow strangers.
3. Choose one measurable goal: qualified Android installs, product feedback or newsletter sign-ups. DR alone is not a launch goal.

### D-7 to D-4

1. After publication approval, upload the walkthrough to YouTube and verify it while logged out.
2. Create the Product Hunt draft after explicit approval and add the direct homepage plus Google Play link.
3. Preview the thumbnail, gallery order, description and first comment on desktop and mobile.
4. Build a small list of real users, collaborators or relevant peers who already know Noctalia. Do not add strangers.

### D-3 to D-1

1. Test homepage, Play link, pricing, privacy, terms and video from an incognito session.
2. Confirm that the maker account is attached and can comment.
3. Ask the approved cohort whether they are willing to try the product and leave honest feedback on launch day. Never request an upvote.
4. Choose the date only when Thanh can respond to comments during the first working hours.

### Launch day

1. Publish at the scheduled `12:01 AM` Pacific boundary.
2. Post the prepared maker comment immediately.
3. Send only the separately approved announcement messages, phrased as feedback requests.
4. Answer every substantive comment promptly and factually. Do not make medical, predictive or privacy claims beyond the public methodology.
5. Record the live Product Hunt URL, referral visits, Play clicks, comments and verified conversions.

### D+1 to D+7

1. Thank contributors without asking them to change votes or reviews.
2. Fix factual misunderstandings in the listing if needed.
3. Verify the live backlink, canonical, indexability and link attributes before adding Product Hunt to backlink results.
4. Write a short launch retrospective based on observed numbers, not leaderboard rank alone.
