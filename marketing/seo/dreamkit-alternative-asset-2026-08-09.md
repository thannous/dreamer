# DreamKit alternative asset — 2026-08-09

This asset adds a fourth dedicated competitor-alternative destination without
creating a competitor account, buying a subscription or transmitting data. It
supports future editorial outreach and internal discovery; it is not evidence
that a backlink, referring domain or DR increase already exists.

## Why this competitor

- DreamKit overlaps directly with Noctalia on Android dream journaling, AI
  interpretation, generated images and lucid-dream features.
- Google Play showed an established public footprint of 100K+ downloads, 4.0
  stars and about 5.38K reviews on August 9, 2026.
- DreamKit has clear strengths that make the comparison useful: PDF export,
  cloud backup, biometric locking and reality-check reminders.
- The former DreamKit iOS identifier now belongs to Arcana, an astrology and
  tarot app. The asset therefore avoids presenting DreamKit as a current iOS
  dream-journal listing.
- Current official operator signals point to Neural Candy LLC and a named
  United States developer. No Russian operator, address or jurisdiction signal
  was found.

## Evidence and comparison boundaries

The page uses current official website, store and privacy-policy evidence. It
credits DreamKit for its established Android footprint, portable PDF export,
cloud backup, app locking and reality-check reminders. It positions Noctalia
only around its verified Android-first voice capture, visual cards, recurring
symbols and guided private reflection.

It does not claim that Noctalia imports DreamKit archives, supports iOS, offers
a full-journal PDF, CSV or JSON export, has equivalent store scale, is
universally cheaper or is superior for every lucid-dreaming routine. Store
counts, ratings and update dates are labeled as an August 9, 2026 snapshot.
