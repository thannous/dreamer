# DreamKit alternative asset — 2026-08-09

This asset adds a fourth dedicated competitor-alternative destination without
creating a competitor account, buying a subscription or transmitting data. It
supports future editorial outreach and internal discovery; it is not evidence
that a backlink, referring domain or DR increase already exists.

## Why this competitor

- DreamKit overlaps directly with Noctalia on Android dream journaling, AI
  interpretation, generated images and lucid-dream features.
- An earlier Google Play snapshot showed 100K+ downloads, 4.0 stars and about
  5.38K reviews on August 9, 2026, but the same package URL returned HTTP 404
  during the final US and French release checks that day. Those figures are
  historical context, not current-availability evidence.
- DreamKit's former listing advertised useful strengths: PDF export, cloud
  backup, biometric locking and reality-check reminders. Existing users should
  verify those functions locally and export before migrating.
- The former DreamKit iOS identifier now belongs to Arcana, an astrology and
  tarot app. The asset therefore avoids presenting DreamKit as a current iOS
  dream-journal listing.
- No Russian operator, address or jurisdiction signal was found. The live
  DreamKit site does not expose enough current legal-operator information to
  confirm its present owner independently. Neural Candy LLC is the US seller of
  Arcana under the former iOS identifier, but Arcana is a different product.

## Evidence and comparison boundaries

The page uses the live official website, the former-store observations and the
privacy policy. It credits DreamKit's historical Android footprint and the
features advertised by its former listing, while stating that no current store
download or price was verified. It positions Noctalia only around its verified
Android-first voice capture, visual cards, recurring symbols and guided private
reflection.

It does not claim that Noctalia imports DreamKit archives, supports iOS, offers
a full-journal PDF, CSV or JSON export, has equivalent store scale, is
universally cheaper or is superior for every lucid-dreaming routine. Store
counts, ratings and update dates are labeled as a historical August 9, 2026
snapshot followed by a same-day 404 finding.
