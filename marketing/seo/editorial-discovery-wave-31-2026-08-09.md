# Editorial discovery wave 31 — 2026-08-09

This read-only pass evaluated one AI-journaling comparison and three startup
directories before any contact or submission. All four fail a required citation,
trust or link-policy gate. No email, form, account, badge, product data,
verification challenge or payment was used.

## Decision table

| Publisher | Evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Limen](https://limen.day/en/blog/best-ai-journaling-apps) | The current comparison discusses Day One, Reflect, Stoic, Rosebud, Reflectly and the publisher's own Limen product. Its only app call to action points to `app.limen.day`; the competing products receive plain-text mentions rather than developer-domain citations. [Current Terms](https://limen.day/en/terminos) identify Alture Digital, S.L. in Spain, select Spanish law and publish `support@limen.day`. | Reject before contact. The accountable non-Russian operator gate passes, but the product-owned page demonstrates no external competitor-domain citation path. |
| [StartupDirectory](https://startupdirectory.net/pricing) | The free tier requires Noctalia to install the directory's badge in exchange for a dofollow link. The no-badge tier costs $9.99. [Current Terms](https://startupdirectory.net/legal/terms) reserve the right to change link attributes and name neither the operator nor a concrete governing jurisdiction. | Reject both routes. The free option is a reciprocal-link scheme and the alternative sells the link; operator country and non-Russian status are also unverified. |
| [Startup List](https://startup-list.org/submit) | The submission route requires an account and promises a permanent backlink. Public sample listings expose followed-looking outbound product links, but the [Terms](https://startup-list.org/terms) identify no legal entity, address or country and define governing law only as the unnamed jurisdiction where the operator is established. The [Privacy Policy](https://startup-list.org/privacy) calls the service an independent publishing project without closing that identity gap. | Reject before account creation or data transfer. Link treatment alone cannot overcome an unaccountable operator and an unverifiable non-Russian gate. |
| [SubmitStartup.io](https://submitstartup.io/) | The free plan explicitly offers only a nofollow link; the $29 featured tier explicitly upgrades it to dofollow. Terms and Privacy publish only the brand and `hello@submitstartup.io`, with no entity, address or jurisdiction. The public tool sitemap exposes six listing URLs, which is insufficient to substantiate the homepage's `500+` listing claim. | Reject. The free route has no authority-transfer value, the followed route sells the link, the public inventory claim is not substantiated by the exposed sitemap and the operator's country is unknown. |

## Measurement boundary

These exclusions create no backlink, referring domain, indexation event,
referral session or DR change. They prevent Noctalia from exchanging a reciprocal
badge, buying followed links or transferring account and product data to
operators whose identity and non-Russian status cannot be established.
