# Media-source backlink pack — 2026-08-09

This pack qualifies five non-Russian, free media-source routes that can lead to
genuine editorial citations. It prepares truthful identity, expertise and reply
copy only. It does not authorize an account, newsletter subscription, public
profile, pitch, mailbox connection, publication or payment.

outcome is an editor-controlled citation on an independent publisher. None
guarantees selection, a backlink, followed treatment or a DR increase.

## Eligible query lanes

Respond only when the query directly fits first-hand experience in:

1. building and operating an independent Android or React Native product;
2. mobile voice capture, transcription UX or morning journaling friction;
3. responsible AI product design, uncertainty language and non-clinical limits;
4. privacy communication for personal journaling or reflective apps;
5. founder lessons from app launches, freemium products and small teams;
6. dream-journaling habits framed as personal experience and product
   observation, never medical or psychological expertise.

Reject health, diagnosis, trauma treatment, sleep-disorder, predictive-dream,
therapeutic-outcome, clinical-efficacy and scientific-expert requests. Also
reject pay-to-publish, reciprocal-link and guaranteed-placement requests.
