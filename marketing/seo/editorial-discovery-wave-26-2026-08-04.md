# Editorial discovery wave 26 — Italian Android diary roundups

Evidence date: 2026-08-04 (Europe/Paris). This was a read-only qualification pass. No account, email, form, attachment, CAPTCHA, product data, payment or publication action was used. Russian-language sites and Russian-operated routes remain excluded.

## Qualification scope

The pass checked Italian-language Android diary and dream-journal results for a current, independent editorial route. A topical article was not counted as a backlink unless the page is currently reachable, the publisher/contact path is usable, the operator gate is acceptable and a future link can be independently verified.

## Decisions

| Candidate | Current evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [XANTARMOB — diary apps for Android](https://xantarmob.altervista.org/le-migliori-applicazioni-diario-per-android/) | The rendered article is dated 17/12/2019 and shows 08/12/2023 as its update date, with the generic `xantarmob` byline. It describes a broad set of diary apps, links directly to downloads and includes an explicit affiliate disclosure. The apparent [/contatti/](https://xantarmob.altervista.org/contatti/) route returned HTTP 404 in the direct check. | Reject as a stale affiliate/download list with no reliable editorial route. No Russian signal was found, but that is not enough to offset the age and missing contact path. No outreach was attempted. |
| [BusinessMakerApp — 20 diary apps for Android](https://businessmakerapp.com/post/le-migliori-apps-diario-per-android.html) | A cached rendered copy reports `Updated at: 27/08/2024`, includes DreamKit at #19 and shows Italian footer addresses plus an obfuscated email. Current direct checks returned HTTP 404 for the article URL and for [/contact/](https://businessmakerapp.com/contact/) and [/contatti/](https://businessmakerapp.com/contatti/). | Reject as currently unavailable and commercially/template-oriented. The Italian footer is not a substitute for a working editorial route; no Russian signal was found, but no safe current submission path exists. No outreach was attempted. |
| [Oniri Italian dream-journal page](https://www.oniri.io/it/dream-journal) | Product-owned competitor page, not an independent publication or directory. | Exclude; do not treat a competitor page as an outreach or backlink prospect. |

## Result

Two P2 exclusions were added to `marketing/seo/backlink-prospects-2026-07-31.csv`, bringing the register to 169 opportunities (2 P0, 57 P1, 109 P2 and 1 P3). This pass produced no new backlink, publication, referring domain or Domain Rating gain. The next useful Italian pass should target an identifiable Android editorial desk or a live Italian developer-submission directory rather than stale SEO listicles.
