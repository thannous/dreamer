# Editorial discovery wave 24 — Android directory safety triage

Evidence date: 2026-08-04 (Europe/Paris). This was a read-only discovery and qualification pass. No account, form, email, attachment, CAPTCHA, APK, product data, payment or publication action was used.

## Search scope

The pass checked official developer/submission pages for non-Russian Android routes and then searched for an existing Noctalia listing. The Russian-language AndroidOut mirror was explicitly kept out of scope; only the English host was considered. A route was not treated as a backlink unless a public Noctalia page, indexability and link treatment could later be verified.

## Decisions

| Candidate | Current evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AndroidOut developer route](https://www.androidout.com/developers/) | The official page says publication is free and that its content team creates an app review. It publishes a Singapore address and company number `201819356K`; the corporate site describes the Android review community. No Noctalia page appeared in the current search. | Record as a conditional, non-Russian-host opportunity only. The wider network exposes a Russian-language mirror, and the developer page does not guarantee an external-site link or followed treatment. Do not use the Russian route; no submission or message was made. |
| [AppsApk submission route](https://www.appsapk.com/submit-app/) | The official page says submissions are free and reviewed by an editorial team. Its Terms identify Tomaya Ltd., Israeli law and a Tel Aviv address. Existing dream-journal pages show topical relevance. | Keep as a low-priority hold: it is an APK mirror, not a specialist editorial publication, and the official-site link/`rel` treatment is not guaranteed. No binary, account or submission was used. |
| [AppAdvice developer route](https://appadvice.com/page/contact-info-developers) | AppAdvice describes itself as an iOS discovery/review site and publishes AppAdvice LLC as operator. No public App Store listing for Noctalia's package was found. | Exclude for platform mismatch; do not pitch an Android-only product. |
| [AppDirectory](https://appdirectory.org/get-listed) | The form accepts only free web apps or free tiers and exposes no accountable operator on the accessible route. Noctalia's mobile app is not a fit for this submission gate. | Exclude; no account or form action. |

## Result

The pass produced one conditional English-host Android directory route and one low-priority APK-mirror hold, but no immediately safe, high-value editorial backlink. Both remain authorization-gated. The tracker records the reasons so the Russian-language route, binary upload and unverified link treatment are not accidentally used later.
