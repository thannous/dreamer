editorial domain-citation opportunity, not merely another product mention or

## Detailed evidence

### DreamScribe

The rendered comparison is transparent about ownership and does not hide
competitor strengths. That editorial disclosure is positive, but it does not
create authority value for Noctalia. Oniri, Dream Journal Ultimate, Elsewhere,
Lucidity and Awoken receive no outbound developer-domain link. The visible
DreamScribe-vs-competitor calls to action stay on `dreamscribeapp.com`, while the
only external app destination is DreamScribe's own Apple listing.

The current Terms define the company only as `DreamScribe`, choose the law and
jurisdiction “where DreamScribe operates” and publish no legal entity, address
or country. The contact address is a support mailbox. This is insufficient to
pass the user's conservative non-Russian operator gate. No contact was made.

### Planwiz

The article says it tested several applications but does not link any competing
dream-journal product. Its visible external editorial links point to Planwiz
templates, Planwiz application routes or the publisher's own app-store pages.
It also gives many health or performance figures without visible supporting
citations, including approximate improvements in recall, memory, anxiety,
nightmares, sleep and PTSD recovery, plus a six-times lucid-dreaming claim.

The public Terms and Privacy documents repeatedly refer to an `Owner`, governed
by the law and courts where that owner is based, but do not identify that owner
or place. This fails both accountability and non-Russian verification. The
combination of unsupported high-stakes claims, own-product-only linking and
opaque ownership makes outreach unsuitable.

### Dream and Meaning

This publisher demonstrates that it can issue direct followed source links, and
its footer identifies `Dream and Meaning` as an AccelerMedia LLC trademark with
a Jacksonville, Florida mailing address. That closes the location gate.
However, its app table sends named products only to Apple or Google stores, not
to developer domains. More importantly, the current About page describes an
unnamed expert team with psychology, neuroscience and spirituality backgrounds,
client work and analysis of an extremely large user set without publishing
verifiable names, credentials or methodology. The same site mixes affiliate
commerce with trauma, therapy, prophetic-dream and premonition content.

Noctalia's non-medical and non-predictive positioning should not be placed into
that context merely to obtain a link. No contact was made.

## Measurement boundary

These are rejected prospects, not backlinks. Adding them to the research
register changes neither the six verified followed pages nor Ahrefs DR. Resume
only if a publisher materially changes the observed external-link policy,
operator disclosure or evidence quality and passes a new current review.
