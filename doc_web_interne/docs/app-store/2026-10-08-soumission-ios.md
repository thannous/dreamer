## Notes pour l'équipe de vérification (à coller en anglais)

> Noctalia is a dream journal. No account is needed: reviewers can tap "Skip" on the introduction and write a dream on the Capture tab. Guest mode includes a few free AI analyses; signing in with Apple or email unlocks more.
>
> AI: dream analysis, the guided conversation and illustrations are generated by a third-party provider (Google Gemini). Before the first AI request the app asks for explicit permission (dialog "AI-powered analysis"); declining keeps the journal fully usable without AI.
>
> Noctalia Plus is an optional auto-renewable subscription (Settings → Noctalia Plus, or after the free analyses are used). Restore Purchases is available on the paywall.
>
> Account deletion (signed-in users): Settings → legal section at the bottom → Delete account.
>
> Apple Health: the binary contains HealthKit usage descriptions because a shared native module is linked, but this app version does not request HealthKit access (it is used only by our separate Lucid Trainer variant).
>
> Noctalia offers prompts for reflection and does not provide medical or psychological diagnosis.

Contact de revue : ton nom, ton téléphone et contact@noctalia.app. Compte de démonstration : inutile grâce au mode invité ; si Apple en demande un, en créer un dédié (ne pas réutiliser ton compte).
