<h2 class="font-serif text-2xl text-dream-cream mb-3">Quick answer</h2>
<p>Check five things: the audio, the AI provider, model training, deletion and advertising. A trustworthy app answers each one plainly in its privacy policy, naming its providers. A vague promise is not an answer.</p>
</div>
<h2>Why is a dream journal sensitive data?</h2>
<p>Even an absurd dream can name a loved one, a breakup, a health worry or a desire. The GDPR does not list dreams as such, but a dream note can hold what it calls special categories: health, sex life, beliefs (Article 9).</p>
<p>Over months, entries add up to a portrait. That is what makes a journal useful, as the <a class="text-dream-salmon hover:underline" href="dreams-emotion-regulation-study-2026">2026 study linking dream emotions with morning mood</a> shows. It should stay readable to you, not vanish into a black box.</p>
<h2>Five checks before choosing an AI journal</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Audio:</strong> recordings serve transcription, then are deleted. Silence on this is a red flag.</li>
<li><strong>AI provider:</strong> named, with what is sent. “Trusted partners” is not a name.</li>
<li><strong>Training:</strong> the app says whether your dreams can train models.</li>
<li><strong>Deletion:</strong> in the app, with a stated timeframe.</li>
<li><strong>Advertising:</strong> no data sale, no targeted ads, in plain words.</li>
</ul>
<h2>How does Noctalia answer these checks?</h2>
<p>Our <a class="text-dream-salmon hover:underline" href="/en/privacy-policy">privacy policy</a> says: speech recognition runs on your phone first; if it fails, audio may go to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text, held a few minutes, never stored by Noctalia. Dream text goes to Google (Gemini) for analysis and images, with training of general models turned off where the service allows. The journal is hosted in the EU. No data sale, no targeted ads. <a class="text-dream-salmon hover:underline" href="/en/account-deletion">Account deletion</a> is in Settings; data is generally erased within 30 days.</p>
<h2>What the EU AI Act changes</h2>
<p>The AI Act, adopted in 2024, has applied in stages since 2025. A dream journal is not among its high-risk uses. If you chat with an AI, you must be told it is a machine. So an “AI Act compliant” badge says little about your notes: access and deletion come from the GDPR.</p>
<h2>Good analysis should stay reversible</h2>
<p>An AI analysis is a reading, not a verdict. Prefer an app that keeps your words apart from the analysis and lets you disagree. Our <a class="text-dream-salmon hover:underline" href="dream-journal-guide">dream journal guide</a> puts your own context first.</p>
