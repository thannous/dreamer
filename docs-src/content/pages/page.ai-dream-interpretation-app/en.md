    <h2 class="text-2xl font-serif text-white mb-4">Two worked examples: a nightmare and a recurring dream</h2>
    <p>Both dreams below are made up. The readings show the kind of reflection you can get, not the app’s word-for-word output.</p>
    <div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-4 mt-6 text-sm">
      <article class="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/5 p-4 space-y-3">
        <h3 class="text-white font-medium">A nightmare</h3>
        <p><strong class="text-purple-200">Dream:</strong> “Someone is following me through a parking garage. I’m looking for my car and every row looks the same. I wake up with a jolt, heart pounding.”</p>
        <p><strong class="text-purple-200">What stands out:</strong> parking garage, a pursuer with no face, identical rows, fear on waking.</p>
        <p><strong class="text-purple-200">Possible readings:</strong> the identical rows could point to a feeling of going in circles. The pursuit could point to pressure whose source you can’t see. Nothing in this dream predicts real danger.</p>
        <p><strong class="text-purple-200">Journal question:</strong> “Do I recognize this feeling of being followed from somewhere else?”</p>
        <p class="text-purple-200/70">Name the fear in your own words: the analysis only picks up emotions you actually write down. If nightmares come back often and disrupt your sleep, talk to a doctor. The <a href="/en/blog/stop-nightmares-guide" class="text-dream-salmon hover:underline">nightmares guide</a> covers what may help.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/5 p-4 space-y-3">
        <h3 class="text-white font-medium">A recurring dream</h3>
        <p><strong class="text-purple-200">Dream:</strong> “That exam dream again. I’m in the lecture hall, I haven’t studied, the page stays blank. Third time this month. I wake up annoyed more than worried.”</p>
        <p><strong class="text-purple-200">What stands out:</strong> exam, blank page, not prepared, the repetition you noted, annoyance.</p>
        <p><strong class="text-purple-200">Possible readings:</strong> the scene could point to a fear of being judged before you feel ready. Annoyance rather than panic shifts that reading: it may be weariness more than fear.</p>
        <p><strong class="text-purple-200">Journal question:</strong> “On the nights this dream comes back, what is waiting for me the next day?”</p>
        <p class="text-purple-200/70">Each analysis starts from the account you give it. So write down that the dream keeps coming back: it can then be marked as recurring, and the Recurring filter in your journal brings those dreams together. When you reread them, look at what changes from one time to the next. The <a href="/en/blog/recurring-dreams-meaning" class="text-dream-salmon hover:underline">recurring dreams guide</a> goes further.</p>
      </article>

  <section class="glass-panel p-8 rounded-2xl border border-white/10 bg-white/5">
    <h2 class="text-2xl font-serif text-white mb-4">How to ask a good follow-up question</h2>
    <p>After the analysis, the conversation stays on the same dream. Pick an angle (Symbols, Emotions, Personal growth) or ask your own question, typed or dictated. The AI only knows the dream and what you tell it, so a good question gives it what it cannot guess.</p>
    <div class="overflow-x-auto mt-6">
      <table class="w-full text-left text-sm min-w-[560px]">
        <thead class="text-purple-200"><tr><th class="py-3 pr-4">Vague question</th><th class="py-3 pr-4">Question that helps</th></tr></thead>
        <tbody class="divide-y divide-white/10">
          <tr><td class="py-4 pr-4">What does my dream mean?</td><td class="py-4 pr-4 text-white">Why am I more annoyed than scared in front of the blank page?</td></tr>
          <tr><td class="py-4 pr-4">What does the parking garage symbolize?</td><td class="py-4 pr-4 text-white">This garage looks like the one at my old job. What readings does that link open?</td></tr>
          <tr><td class="py-4 pr-4">Does this dream predict something?</td><td class="py-4 pr-4 text-white">This dream left me feeling I was going in circles. What could I pay attention to this week?</td></tr>
        </tbody>
      </table>
    </div>
    <ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mt-6">
      <li><strong>Add your own associations.</strong> A place, a person, something from the day before: that is what makes a reading personal.</li>
      <li><strong>Correct what feels wrong.</strong> “No, I wasn’t scared, I was annoyed.” The rest of the conversation takes it into account.</li>
      <li><strong>Ask for more when you want it.</strong> Replies are short by default. Write “go deeper” or “give me an example”.</li>
    </ul>
    <p class="mt-4">Each dream has a cap on exchanges: up to 10 follow-up messages per analyzed dream on the free plan. With Plus, chat is unlimited under fair use, up to 100 follow-up messages per dream. Precise questions get more out of them.</p>
