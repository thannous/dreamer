<h1 class="font-serif text-4xl md:text-5xl mb-4 text-dream-cream">DreamKit alternative after its store listings changed</h1>
<p class="text-sm text-purple-300/70 mb-6">Reviewed August 9, 2026</p>
<p class="text-lg text-purple-200/80 leading-relaxed mb-10">DreamKit was a longstanding dream journal whose former Android listing advertised PDF export, cloud backup, AI interpretation, images and reality-check reminders. That Google Play URL returned 404 during release verification on August 9, 2026, while its former iOS identifier now opens Arcana. Noctalia is an Android alternative you can download today; this page compares the two and shows how to move your dreams carefully.</p>
